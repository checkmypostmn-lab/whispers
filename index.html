About the author

Sainbileg D is a Mongolian writer and painter. Sainbileg writes poetry and fairy tales about the quiet things people carry inside: thoughts, words, and the shapes they take.

Whispers of the Hidden World began almost ten years ago in Mongolian. The watercolor cover was painted by the author.
